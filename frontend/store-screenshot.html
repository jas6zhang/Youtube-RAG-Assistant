Why is self-attention better than an RNN for long sequences?
Answer: Self-attention lets the model relate every token to every other token in a single step, so long-range dependencies are captured directly instead of being passed along step-by-step. It also removes the sequential bottleneck of RNNs, making training far more parallelizable.
